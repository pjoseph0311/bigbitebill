ABOUT ME

Why I cook

“Cooking is the one place where I get to make everything my own.”

When I moved away for college, cooking was just survival. For a couple of weeks I ate bacon and eggs, and that was it. Somewhere along the way it stopped being a chore. Prepping food turned into how I unwind after a long day, and I started enjoying the process of learning recipes.

Honestly, a lot of it started in class. I’d be sitting there hungry, scrolling Instagram reels, and by the time I got home I wanted to make something. My roommates became my first customers, and cooking for my friends made it fun instead of a necessity.

These days I don’t stick to recipes. I’ll learn one, then bend it until it feels like mine, and that personal touch is my favorite part. Grilled chicken, brisket, and mussels are some of my favorites to make. But what I like most is feeding people, and cooking for the people around me is the whole point.
